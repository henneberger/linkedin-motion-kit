# Context graph motion slideshow

## Story

The graph is not just for retrospective search. Prediction exposes whether the graph contains enough relationships to support a decision.

The slideshow uses six scenes:

1. **The graph isn’t just for search.**
2. **Thin context versus rich context.**
3. **Prediction stress-tests the graph.**
4. **Churn, fraud, expansion, and upgrade decisions.**
5. **Search explains the past; prediction anticipates the next.**
6. **Build the graph around the decision—not the query.**

All typography is rendered by the video engine rather than baked into the generated image.
The generated background remains spatially locked. Motion is applied to graph relationships, packets, endpoint routes, and the decision core; jitter is limited to short text impacts.
